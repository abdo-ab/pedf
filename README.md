## Recommended execution

Do not give the coding agent all ten prompts at once.

Give Part 1 first. After it completes and tests successfully, provide Part 2, and continue sequentially.

Part 7–10 are the critical PDF-engine phases. Do not proceed to SaaS payments/admin until the end-to-end PDF editing workflow works.
